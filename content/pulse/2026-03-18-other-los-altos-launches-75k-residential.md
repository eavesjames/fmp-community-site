## What it is

Los Altos launched a $75,000 city-funded rebate program administered by Silicon Valley Clean Energy to incentivize residential conversion from gas to electric water heaters and heat-pump HVAC units during home renovations. The program aims to advance building electrification and municipal environmental goals.

## Why it matters

Contractors and homeowners renovating homes face coordination between city rebates, CCA incentives, and electrification timelines. For MEP contractors, stacked incentive programs can influence equipment sizing, panel upgrade decisions, and project economics during residential retrofit windows.

**Evidence from source:**

- $75,000 city allocation for residential electrification rebates, targeting gas-to-electric water heaters and heat-pump HVAC conversions during renovations
- Administered by Silicon Valley Clean Energy (SVCE), which already offers its own rebates and serves 96% of local residents/businesses
- Program unanimously approved by City Council in December, went live March 2026
