## What it is

ABB-sponsored webinar led by Don Kiper (Estimating 101) presents a four-step electrical estimating framework emphasizing systematic takeoffs, labor unit considerations, and bid accuracy. Content promotes ABB's ReliaGear smart power distribution line as a tool to improve project efficiency and contractor profit.

## Why it matters

Electrical contractors preparing bids for power distribution projects face labor estimation errors and schedule risk. A standardized estimating sequence and attention to labor adjustments help contractors enter agreements with confidence in project profitability. Products that reduce installation complexity can improve bidding competitiveness.

**Evidence from source:**

- Don Kiper has 42 years' experience as electrician, estimator, and project manager; writes 'Estimating Essentials' column for EC&M magazine
- ReliaGear smart power distribution products claim to 'lower installation costs, improve project efficiency, and increase profit'
- Webinar emphasizes 'labor units and labor adjustments' as critical estimating considerations
