## What it is

Panduit announces the launch of the first UL-certified Fault Managed Power System for Class 4 power. The post emphasizes that Panduit helped shape the UL certification process itself, not just meet existing standards. The system aims to enable intelligent infrastructure powered safely, compliantly, and at scale.

## Why it matters

UL certification for Class 4 FMP systems directly addresses code/AHJ friction that has historically delayed deployments. Electrical contractors and inspectors now have a recognized compliance pathway for intelligent fault-managed power installations. This removes a critical approval bottleneck for projects requiring safe, monitored low-voltage distribution beyond traditional Class 2 limits.

- Panduit launched 'the first UL-certified Fault Managed Power System' for Class 4 power

- System described as enabling 'intelligent infrastructure...powered safely, compliantly, and at scale'
