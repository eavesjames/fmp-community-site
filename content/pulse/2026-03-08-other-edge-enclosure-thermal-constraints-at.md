## What it is

A Facebook post from Chatsworth Products mentions that as power density increases in edge computing environments to 5–10 kW per enclosure, the enclosure itself becomes a thermal constraint.

## Why it matters

Edge deployments reaching 5–10 kW per enclosure face thermal bottlenecks that affect enclosure selection and cooling strategy. Facilities and IT-network teams must account for heat dissipation when sizing power distribution and ventilation. This impacts edge site design and may require active cooling or alternative enclosure solutions.

**Evidence from source:**

- Heat loads approach 5–10 kW per edge enclosure, making the enclosure a thermal constraint
