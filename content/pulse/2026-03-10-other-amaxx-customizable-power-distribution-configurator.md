## What it is

Mennekes AMAXX power distribution configurator allows specification of switched and interlocked pin-and-sleeve receptacles in combinations up to 10 devices. Supports 20A-60A at voltages from 120/208V through 600V with 2-5 wire configurations and optional GFCI.

## Why it matters

Switched and interlocked receptacles address lockout/tagout and equipment sequencing in edge and industrial environments. Electrical contractors need fast spec tools for multi-voltage jobs, but the excerpt provides no detail on interlock behavior, failure modes, UL listing status, or installation constraints that would inform design or labor decisions.

**Evidence from source:**

- Supports 20A, 30A, and 60A receptacles with voltages 120/208V, 125V, 125/250V, 250V, 480V, 600V
- Maximum 10 combined devices per configuration; 60A receptacles require 2 modules
- GFCI receptacles limited to 125V, 2 Pole, 3 Wire, maximum 5 per box
