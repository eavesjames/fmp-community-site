## What it is

Panduit announces the launch of the first UL-certified Fault Managed Power System for Class 4 power. The post emphasizes Panduit's involvement in shaping the UL certification standards themselves, positioning the solution for compliant deployment of intelligent infrastructure.

## Why it matters

UL certification for Class 4 FMP systems removes a critical AHJ approval barrier that has slowed adoption. Electrical contractors and integrators can now specify Fault Managed Power with standardized safety compliance, affecting design decisions for edge infrastructure and intelligent building deployments. The establishment of a formal certification pathway signals maturation of the technology for scaled rollout.

- First UL-certified Fault Managed Power System launched by Panduit
- Panduit participated in shaping the UL certification process for Class 4 power
- System positioned for powering intelligent infrastructure 'safely, compliantly, and at scale'
