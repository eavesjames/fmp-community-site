## What it is

The City of Copperas Cove municipal government announced adoption of the 2024 International Code Council building codes, which incorporate NFPA 70 NEC 2023 edition. The post specifically highlights the inclusion of new Article 726 covering Class 4 Fault-Managed Power Systems.

## Why it matters

Code adoption at the municipal AHJ level directly impacts electrical contractors and integrators seeking permits for FMP installations. Explicit callout of Article 726 in the adoption announcement suggests local building officials are aware of the new standard, potentially streamlining plan review and inspection processes for projects using fault-managed power systems.

**Evidence from source:**

- City adopts 2024 ICC codes incorporating NFPA 70 NEC 2023 edition
- Adoption includes Article 726 — Class 4 Fault-Managed Power Systems, explicitly called out as 'new Article'
