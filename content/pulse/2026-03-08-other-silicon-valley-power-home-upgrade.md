## What it is

Silicon Valley Power municipal utility rebate program rules for residential electrification. Program provides $5,500 base rebate for gas-to-electric heat pump water heater conversions and explicitly covers the cost of electrical upgrades required to support the new appliances.

## Why it matters

Electrical service and panel upgrades are a known retrofit barrier for residential electrification. This rebate program explicitly covers electrical upgrade costs alongside appliance rebates, reducing the financial friction for electrical contractors and homeowners navigating gas-to-electric conversions. The before-photo requirement suggests compliance and verification mechanisms for rebate processing.

**Evidence from source:**

- Heat Pump Water Heater (gas-to-electric) Base Rebate Amount: $5,500
- Rebate covers 'cost of the electrical upgrade(s)' required for conversion
- 'Before' Photo requirement for previous appliance suggests verification process
