## What it is

EC&M Code Basics column by Mike Holt covers NEC Article 300 requirements for wiring methods, including mandatory neutral pigtailing for multiwire branch circuits, minimum 6-inch conductor length for splices, prohibition of splices in raceways except wireways, and raceway sizing rules per Annex C.

## Why it matters

Electrical contractors must pigtail neutrals per Sec. 300.13(B) to prevent neutral interruption hazards when devices are removed, adding splicing labor but eliminating a common failure mode. The 6-inch free conductor requirement (Sec. 300.14) and raceway fill limits directly constrain box sizing, pull planning, and labor productivity during rough-in. Misapplication of fittings or splice locations triggers inspection failures and rework.

**Evidence from source:**

- Sec. 300.13(B) requires neutral conductor continuity via splicing and pigtail; pigtailing not permitted with GFCI devices due to current sensing.
- Sec. 300.14 mandates at least 6 inches of conductor measured from box entry point, with 3 inches outside box for openings <8 inches.
- Sec. 300.15 prohibits conductor splices in raceways except wireways per Sec. 376.56; boxes required at splice/termination points with listed exceptions.
