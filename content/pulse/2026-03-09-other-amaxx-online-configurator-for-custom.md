## What it is

Online configuration tool for AMAXX® power distribution systems supporting pin-and-sleeve receptacles (20-60A, multiple voltages/wire configs) and GFCI receptacles with various enclosure ratings. Users specify device quantities and parameters; sales engineers provide quotes.

## Why it matters

- GFCI options include weatherproof and Type 4X enclosures
