## What it is

Panduit announces the launch of the first UL-certified Fault Managed Power System for Class 4 power, emphasizing their participation in creating the UL certification standards. The post positions this as enabling safe, compliant intelligent infrastructure deployment at scale.

## Why it matters

UL certification for Class 4 FMP systems addresses a critical AHJ approval barrier that has slowed adoption. Electrical contractors and integrators now have a certified product pathway that reduces code interpretation debates during permitting and commissioning. This milestone affects design decisions by establishing compliance precedent for intelligent, digitally-managed power distribution.

- Panduit launched 'the first UL-certified Fault Managed Power System' for Class 4 power
- Panduit claims they 'helped create' the UL certification process for Class 4 power
- Post references powering 'intelligent infrastructure...safely, compliantly, and at scale'
