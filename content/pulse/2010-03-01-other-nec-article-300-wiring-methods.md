## What it is

This article explains NEC Article 300, which provides foundational rules for installing conductors and raceways covered in Chapter 3. It addresses requirements for conductor grouping, parallel runs, equipment grounding conductor placement, inductive heating mitigation in nonferrous raceways, and protection from physical damage through wooden and metal framing members.

## Why it matters

Electrical contractors and MEP designers must understand Article 300 requirements to ensure compliant installations across all wiring methods. The excerpt details specific constraints like the 1.25-inch clearance rule for holes in wooden framing and mandatory metal plate protection, which directly affect rough-in labor, material takeoffs, and inspection approval. Knowledge of parallel conductor rules per 310.4 and EGC placement exceptions per 250.130(C) impacts both new construction and retrofit pathway design.

**Evidence from source:**

- Holes through wooden framing must be at least 1.25 inches from the front edge or require metal plate protection per 300.4(A)
- All conductors of a circuit must be in the same raceway/cable/trench per 300.3, with four exceptions in 300.3(B)(1)-(4)
- Equipment grounding conductors can be located outside flexible raceway if bonding jumper installed per 250.102(E)
