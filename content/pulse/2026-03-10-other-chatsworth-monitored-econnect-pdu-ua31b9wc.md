## What it is

Chatsworth's UA-31B9W-C is a monitored PDU supporting up to 100A input and 34.5kW, with remote monitoring at input and branch circuit levels. Features include Secure Array networking (single IP for 48 PDUs), 149°F temperature rating for hot-aisle mounting, phase-balanced outlets on three-phase models, patented locking outlets, and UL/IEC 62368 compliance.

## Why it matters

Facilities teams deploying GPU and AI workloads face power density challenges requiring high-amperage PDUs (100A, 30A branch breakers) and cabinet-level monitoring to manage load balancing and prevent outages. The 149°F rating and hot-aisle tolerance directly address thermal constraints in dense racks, while Secure Array topology reduces network complexity and IP overhead. UL 62368 compliance affects procurement and AHJ approval timelines for new deployments.
