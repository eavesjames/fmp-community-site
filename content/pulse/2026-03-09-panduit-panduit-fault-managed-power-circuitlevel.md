## What it is

Panduit's Fault Managed Power System provides intelligent power distribution that monitors and isolates electrical faults at the individual circuit level, disconnecting only affected circuits in under 4 milliseconds. The system prevents cascading failures, reduces arc flash hazards, and offers remote monitoring capabilities for data centers, healthcare, financial institutions, and manufacturing operations.

## Why it matters

Traditional breaker panels create all-or-nothing fault scenarios where single failures cascade across equipment branches. Facilities managers and electrical contractors gain granular fault management that maintains uptime for unaffected circuits, simplifies code compliance through built-in safety features, and avoids expensive electrical service upgrades in distributed IT infrastructure. This shifts the safety model from reactive branch-level protection to proactive circuit-level isolation with sub-5ms response.

**Evidence from source:**

- Automatic disconnection of faulty circuits in under 4 milliseconds with circuit-level monitoring and real-time fault detection
- Significant reduction in arc flash hazards compared to traditional breaker panels, with simplified electrical code compliance through built-in safety features
- Eliminates need for expensive electrical upgrades while providing remote monitoring and management for facility-wide visibility across telecommunications rooms and distributed IT
