## What it is

Educational article covering NEC Article 300 general wiring method requirements including conductor installation in raceways, circuit conductor grouping rules (all conductors including neutral and ground must run together with exceptions for parallel runs), voltage mixing constraints, physical damage protection, and underground installation minimum cover depths per Table 300.5.

## Why it matters

Electrical contractors and facilities teams must navigate Article 300 grouping and routing constraints during both new installations and retrofits. The requirement that all conductors of a circuit run in the same raceway (with specific exceptions for parallel underground nonmetallic runs per 300.5(I)) directly affects conduit sizing, labor planning, and installation schedules. Physical damage protection requirements (300.4) and underground cover depth tables create pathway design constraints that impact material choices and trenching scope.

**Evidence from source:**

- All conductors of a circuit (including neutral and equipment grounding conductors) must be run in the same raceway, cable, trench, cord, or cable tray per Sec. 300.3(B) with specific exceptions.
- Cables and raceways installed underground must have minimum cover per Table 300.5 [Sec. 300.5(A)].
- Parallel phase and neutral conductors can be installed in individual underground nonmetallic raceways (Phase A in raceway 1, Phase B in raceway 2, etc.) per Sec. 300.5(I) Exception No. 2.
