## What it is

The Electrical Association offers self-paced and live continuing education courses approved in multiple states (MN, ND, SD, NE, WY, CO, MT) covering 2026 NEC code updates. Course catalog explicitly includes review of Class 4 Fault-Managed Power Systems as part of their code training curriculum.

## Why it matters

Electrical contractors and licensed electricians need state-approved continuing education to maintain licenses; the inclusion of Class 4 FMP in multi-state approved curriculum indicates code adoption is driving formal training requirements. This training availability directly affects contractor readiness to bid, design, and install FMP systems under 2026 NEC Article 726 and related AHJ approvals.

**Evidence from source:**

- Course approved in MN, ND, SD, NE, WY, CO, MT for continuing education credit
- Class 4 Fault-Managed Power Systems explicitly listed as reviewed topic in curriculum
- Courses tied to 2026 NEC Code Updates training program
