## What it is

HVAC contractor Matt Schlegel explains that switching from gas to electric heat pumps requires electrical infrastructure upgrades that are often overlooked in project planning. Upgrading electrical service, running new wiring, and coordinating with utilities can become the most expensive component, leading some projects to choose dual-fuel systems as a middle ground.

## Why it matters

Building owners and MEP contractors face hidden cost and schedule impacts when existing electrical service cannot support new electric heating loads. This service upgrade constraint directly affects retrofit feasibility decisions and technology selection between full electrification versus hybrid approaches.

**Evidence from source:**

- Switching from gas to electric backup heat requires 'upgrading electrical service, running new wiring, and working with the utility company'
- Electrical infrastructure upgrades are 'often the most expensive part of the entire project'
- Dual fuel systems presented as 'practical middle ground' when electrical upgrades become cost-prohibitive
