## What it is

Panduit became the first company to achieve UL 1400-1 certification for Fault Managed Power Systems, collaborating directly with UL Solutions to develop testing procedures for Class 4 power. The certification validates fault detection, power limitation, and thermal management at the system level, addressing fire and electrical hazard risks.

## Why it matters

UL 1400-1 certification directly impacts project timelines by accelerating AHJ approvals and reducing permitting delays for FMPS installations. Electrical contractors and facility managers face less project risk when using UL-listed systems. Panduit's role in co-developing the standard establishes the safety benchmark for the entire fault managed power industry.

**Evidence from source:**

- UL 1400-1 is the first standard developed specifically for Fault Managed Power Systems, focusing on system-level safety including fault detection, power limitation, and thermal management
- Panduit became the world's first company to earn UL 1400-1 certification for FMPS and collaborated directly with UL Solutions to define testing procedures and safety thresholds
- UL-listed FMP systems simplify permitting and inspections, leading to fewer delays, faster approvals, and less risk for contractors and facility managers per Bob Voss
