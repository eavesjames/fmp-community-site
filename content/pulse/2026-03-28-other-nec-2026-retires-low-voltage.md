## What it is

NEC 2026 eliminates the ambiguous term 'low voltage' in favor of 'limited energy,' ending Chapter 8's historical code independence for communications systems. High-power PoE and Class 4 fault-managed power are now formally defined within the limited energy framework and subject to the full code structure.

## Why it matters

Electrical contractors and designers must now treat PoE, high-power PoE, and Class 4 fault-managed power systems under unified code requirements (Chapters 1-7), eliminating the previous exemption for communications systems. This affects plan review, AHJ permitting, labeling requirements, and how mixed-system spaces and pathways are designed and inspected.

**Evidence from source:**

- NEC 2026 introduces formal definition for Limited Energy Cable in Article 100, replacing 'low voltage' terminology
- Chapter 8's historical independence removed; communications systems no longer exempt from Chapters 1–7
- High-power PoE and Class 4 fault-managed power explicitly situated within limited energy framework
