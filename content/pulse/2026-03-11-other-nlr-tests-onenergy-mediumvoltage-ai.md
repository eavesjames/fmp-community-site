## What it is

ON.energy is testing a medium-voltage AI UPS at NLR's ARIES platform, which simulates both data centers and grids. The technology aims to manage sub-second power spikes, voltage anomalies, and blackout ride-through for hyperscale data centers consuming city-scale energy (e.g., Boulder, CO equivalent).

## Why it matters

Hyperscale data centers create sub-second load swings that can spike or plummet multiple times per minute, stressing grid interconnects and traditional UPS architectures. Facilities and utility engineers must evaluate medium-voltage UPS topologies to manage these transients, balance grid-interactive capabilities, and meet resilience requirements without overbuilding infrastructure. The ARIES testing validates design assumptions before live deployment.

**Evidence from source:**

- AI UPS described as 'medium-voltage' design, explicitly novel architecture requiring isolated testing before grid deployment.
- Data center power can 'spike or plummet in less than a second, ping-pong from one extreme to another multiple times in under a minute, or zero-out without warning.'
- Single data center consumes energy equivalent to Boulder, CO; 70% of future demand will be hyperscale (hyper-massive) facilities.
