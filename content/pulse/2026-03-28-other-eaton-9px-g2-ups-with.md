## What it is

Short promotional video showcasing Eaton's 9PX G2 UPS system with lithium-ion battery technology. Claims include longer runtimes, extended battery lifespan, and 3x faster charging compared to traditional lead acid batteries for rack-mounted infrastructure.

## Why it matters

Faster recharge cycles (3x vs lead acid) could reduce vulnerability windows during sequential outages in edge sites with limited generator backup. IT/facilities teams spec'ing rack UPS must evaluate lithium-ion TCO tradeoffs against upfront cost and thermal management constraints in small IT closets.

**Evidence from source:**

- 9PX G2 systems feature 'longer runtimes and battery lifespans while charging 3x faster than lead acid batteries'
- Product positioned for 'rack infrastructure' and 'edge computing setup' applications
