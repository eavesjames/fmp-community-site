## What it is

Article explains NEC Article 690 Part IV wiring requirements for photovoltaic installations, focusing on conductor selection and installation methods. Covers allowances for USE-2 and PV wire single conductors with quick-connect plugs, polarity marking alternatives to color-coding per 200.6(A)(6), and wire management challenges for 25+ year system lifespans.

## Why it matters

Electrical contractors installing PV systems must navigate NEC-specific allowances like exposed single-conductor cables and non-color-coded polarity marking that differ from standard wiring practices. These code provisions directly affect field installation methods, conductor procurement (12-10 AWG with quick-connects), and long-term reliability planning for harsh rooftop environments where wire management becomes critical over 25-year operational periods.

**Evidence from source:**

- 690.31(A) allows exposed single-conductor cables (USE-2, PV wire) for circuits within PV arrays, typically 12 AWG or 10 AWG with quick-connect plugs
- 200.6(A)(6) permits non-color-coded conductors if polarity is marked at all terminations; black conductors preferred for UV resistance
- Installations planned for 25+ year lifespan with minimal maintenance in harsh rooftop environments require upfront wire management strategy
