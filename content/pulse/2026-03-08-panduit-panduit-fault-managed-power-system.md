## What it is

Panduit's Fault Managed Power System monitors and isolates faults at the individual circuit level, disconnecting only affected circuits in under 4 milliseconds while maintaining power to adjacent equipment. The system provides remote monitoring, reduces arc flash hazards, and eliminates the need for expensive electrical infrastructure upgrades in data centers and mission-critical facilities.

## Why it matters

Traditional breaker-based distribution creates single points of failure where one fault takes down entire branches. Facilities managers and data center operators face a tradeoff: Panduit's per-circuit isolation prevents cascading outages and reduces arc flash risk, but excerpt lacks pricing, integration constraints, or retrofit complexity details needed for capital planning and design decisions.

**Evidence from source:**

- Automatic disconnection of faulty circuits in under 4 milliseconds while maintaining power to all other connected equipment
- Circuit-level monitoring with real-time fault detection and isolation; significant reduction in arc flash hazards compared to traditional breaker panels
- Eliminates the need for expensive electrical upgrades while providing superior protection and monitoring capabilities
