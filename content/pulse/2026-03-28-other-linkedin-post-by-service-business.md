## What it is

Matthew Schlegel from Bosch explains that building owners converting from gas to electric heat pumps face surprising costs in electrical infrastructure. Panel upgrades, new wiring circuits, and utility company coordination often become the most expensive project component, making dual-fuel systems a practical compromise.

## Why it matters

Facilities managers and building owners planning electrification retrofits must scope electrical distribution upgrades—not just HVAC equipment—to avoid budget surprises. The constraint of existing panel capacity and service sizing directly affects project feasibility, timelines (utility involvement), and the design choice between full electric vs. dual-fuel configurations.

**Evidence from source:**

- Switching from gas to electric backup heat requires 'upgrading electrical service, running new wiring, and working with the utility company'
- Electrical upgrades include 'Panel upgrades, New circuits, And sometimes even utility coordination' and 'ends up being the most expensive part of the entire project'
- Dual fuel positioned as 'practical middle ground' and 'realistic compromise' to avoid full electrical infrastructure overhaul
