## What it is

Houston electrical contractor guide addresses residential panel upgrade decision criteria, costs, and process. Covers when homeowners need service upgrades (adding EV chargers, heat pumps, induction ranges), typical 100-200 amp upgrade costs ($1,600-$4,000+), and City of Houston permitting requirements.

## Why it matters

Service panel capacity is the primary electrical infrastructure constraint for residential electrification retrofits. Electrical contractors face permit requirements, customer pricing expectations ($1,600-$4,000 typical range), and must scope upgrades triggered by 240V loads like Level 2 EV chargers and 2024+ heat pumps. Panel age (25-30 years) and existing capacity determine whether adding high-power appliances requires full service upgrade or panel relocation.

**Evidence from source:**

- Typical Houston panel upgrades in 2026 run approximately $1,600–$4,000 for 100–200 amp service; projects requiring relocation or major rewiring can exceed $5,000
- Plans to install 240-volt appliances (EV charger, 2024+ heat pump, induction range, pool equipment) trigger upgrade need
- All panel work must be performed by a licensed electricator and permitted through the City of Houston Permitting Center
