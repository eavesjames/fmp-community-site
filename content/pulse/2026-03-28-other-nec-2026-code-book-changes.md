## What it is

A Facebook post in an electricians' group announces the NEC 2026 code book release, specifically calling out new Article 726 covering Class 4 Fault-Managed Power Systems. The post also mentions changes to Article 220 for branch circuits, feeders, and service calculations.

## Why it matters

The introduction of Article 726 in NEC 2026 establishes the first formal code framework for Class 4 Fault-Managed Power Systems, directly impacting electrical contractors and AHJs who must navigate compliance for FMP installations. Changes to foundational articles like 220 may also affect how branch circuits and feeders are calculated for FMP deployments.
