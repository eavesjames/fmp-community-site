## What it is

The 2026 ATIS Protection Engineers Group conference convenes network power and protection professionals to address Class 4 Fault Managed Power Systems (NEC Article 726, UL 1400), evolving NFPA 855 battery fire codes, and telecommunications infrastructure design. Recent webinars covered FMPS deployment and battery safety strategies; VoltServer's Luke Getto won 2025 Best Presenter for FMPS installation/maintenance content.

## Why it matters

Telecom and edge facility designers must navigate new Class 4 FMPS standards (NEC 726/UL 1400) and changing NFPA 855 battery deployment rules, directly impacting AHJ approval, installation pathways, and safety protocols. The conference aggregates technical guidance on grounding, fault-managed systems, and battery fire code compliance—critical constraints for network power engineering. Content targets protection engineers responsible for designing code-compliant, resilient power distribution in critical communications infrastructure.
