## What it is

Educational article covering NEC Article 300 wiring method requirements, focusing on raceway securing/support rules, suspended ceiling restrictions, mechanical continuity between boxes/cabinets, and raceway fill calculations using Chapter 9 Table 1 and Annex C.

## Why it matters

Electrical contractors and MEP teams must comply with NEC 300.11(A) prohibition on using suspended ceiling support wires for raceways, requiring independent support wires that must be color-coded or tagged in fire-rated assemblies. This affects pathway installation labor, material costs, and AHJ inspection friction, particularly in retrofit scenarios with existing suspended ceiling grids.

**Evidence from source:**

- NEC 300.11(A): Suspended ceiling support wires cannot support raceways; independent support wires secured at both ends are required
- Fire-rated assemblies require independent support wires to be distinguishable by color, tagging, or other means [300.11(A)(1)]
- Raceway fill limited to 60% for runs ≤24 inches; percentages in Chapter 9 Table 1 apply for mixed conductor sizes/insulation
