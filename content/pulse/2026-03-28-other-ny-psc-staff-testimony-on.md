## What it is

Staff testimony document filed with the New York Public Service Commission discussing the Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act (CLCPA) and building decarbonization. References Local Law 97 of 2019, which requires buildings over 25,000 square feet in New York City to meet greenhouse gas emissions limits.

## Why it matters

Local Law 97 creates compliance pressure for building owners and operators to reduce emissions, potentially accelerating electrification and HVAC replacement projects. However, the PDF excerpt contains no concrete implementation pathways, electrical infrastructure constraints, panel upgrade requirements, or timeline details that would inform design or retrofit decisions.

**Evidence from source:**

- Local Law 97 of 2019 (LL97) requires most buildings over 25,000 square feet in New York City to meet GHG emissions limits
