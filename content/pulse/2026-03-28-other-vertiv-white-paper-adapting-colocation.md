## What it is

Vertiv white paper addresses infrastructure challenges colocation providers face deploying AI workloads. Covers power density growth (40-100 kW to 100-250 kW pods), GPU variable power demands vs. stable CPU loads, and the need for adaptive power/cooling systems to maintain service level agreements.

## Why it matters

Colocation operators must adapt power distribution and UPS architectures to handle GPU clusters with rapidly fluctuating loads (not stable CPU patterns) and densities reaching 250 kW per pod. This affects design decisions around modular power delivery, cooling scalability, and SLA-compliant resilience as AI workloads compress deployment timelines and introduce new skillset gaps.

**Evidence from source:**

- AI pods projected to grow from 40-100 kW to over 100-250 kW in coming years
- GPUs exhibit rapidly fluctuating power usage patterns unlike stable CPU loads
- Paper provides guidance on adapting power systems for AI's unique requirements including adaptive UPS and power delivery
