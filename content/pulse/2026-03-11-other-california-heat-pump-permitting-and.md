## What it is

California's 2026 legislative session includes SB 222 (Heat Pump Access Act) to streamline and modernize heat pump permitting statewide, making it more transparent and affordable. AB 2313 would give customers electrification incentives when gas service lines need replacement. AB 2088 would authorize utilities to build thermal energy network systems for neighborhood-scale heating/cooling.

## Why it matters

SB 222 directly addresses permitting bottlenecks that slow heat pump deployment—making processes 'more transparent, navigable, and affordable for contractors and customers.' AB 2313 creates a new conversion pathway at gas infrastructure end-of-life, potentially concentrating panel upgrade and service work. Electrical contractors and MEP firms face shifting compliance requirements and new scope triggered by utility infrastructure replacement cycles.

**Evidence from source:**

- SB 222 (Heat Pump Access Act) aims to 'modernize and streamline heat pump permitting statewide, making the process more transparent, navigable, and affordable for contractors and customers'
- AB 2313 would require gas utilities to offer customers 'a financial incentive to electrify their home' when gas service lines are due for replacement
- AB 2088 would establish thermal energy network systems (TENs) as 'neighborhood-scale systems use shared underground water pipes to efficiently heat and cool buildings'
