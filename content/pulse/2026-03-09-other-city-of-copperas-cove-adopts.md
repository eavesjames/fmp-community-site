## What it is

The City of Copperas Cove, Texas announced adoption of 2024 International Code Council building codes, which include NFPA 70 NEC 2023 edition. The announcement specifically mentions Article 726 covering Class 4 Fault-Managed Power Systems as a new article.

## Why it matters

Municipal code adoption is the critical gate for permitting FMP installations. Electrical contractors and design-build teams need effective dates, inspection protocols, and AHJ interpretation guidance to bid and schedule projects confidently. The excerpt provides no implementation timeline or enforcement details.
