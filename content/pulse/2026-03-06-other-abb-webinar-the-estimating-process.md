## What it is

ABB webinar featuring Don Kiper (Estimating 101) covers four-step electrical estimating process emphasizing labor considerations and systematic takeoffs. Promotes ReliaGear smart power distribution products as tools to lower installation costs, improve efficiency, and increase profit margins for electrical contractors.

## Why it matters

Electrical contractors bidding power distribution projects face labor estimation complexity and installation efficiency constraints. A standardized estimating sequence combined with products designed to reduce installation burden directly affects bid accuracy, project profitability, and schedule risk. The focus on labor units and adjustments addresses a core constraint in electrical project delivery.

**Evidence from source:**

- ReliaGear smart power distribution products lower installation costs, improve project efficiency, and increase profit
- Estimators must consider labor units and labor adjustments when preparing bids
- Don Kiper has 42 years experience as electrician, estimator, estimating manager, and project manager; trained 170+ estimators
