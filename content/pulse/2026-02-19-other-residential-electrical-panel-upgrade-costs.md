## What it is

Consumer-focused guide explaining residential electrical panel upgrade costs in 2026, typically $2,500–$3,500 for standard upgrades and $2,000–$4,000 for 100A to 200A conversions. Drivers include EV chargers, modern appliance loads, and outdated panels unable to handle concurrent demand.

Residential panel capacity bottlenecks (100→200A) signal broader electrification retrofit demand, relevant to electrical contractors pricing panel work. - 200 amp upgrade total cost $2,300–$4,500 including permit ($150–$500), utility coordination ($200–$1,000), and grounding upgrades ($300–$1,200)

- Installation usually takes one day per SERP snippet
