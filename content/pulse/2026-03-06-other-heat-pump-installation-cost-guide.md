## What it is

Comprehensive guide on heat pump installation costs ranging from $4,000 to $20,000+, emphasizing electrical panel upgrades, ductwork modifications, and labor components. Highlights that leaky ducts waste up to 30% of conditioned air and that panel service upgrades (100-amp to 200-amp) are common requirements for heat pump installations.

## Why it matters

Electrical service upgrades from 100A to 200A are explicitly called out as part of 'more involved installations,' representing a critical constraint for electrical contractors and facilities managers planning heat pump retrofits. This touches the pathway/install bottleneck that affects scheduling, permitting, and cost estimation in building electrification projects.

**Evidence from source:**

- More involved installations include electrical panel upgrades from 100-amp to 200-amp service
- Installation costs range from $4,000 to over $20,000 with biggest swings from three factors
- Leaky ducts waste up to 30% of conditioned air
