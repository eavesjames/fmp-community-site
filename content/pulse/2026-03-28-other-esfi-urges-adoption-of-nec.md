## What it is

The Electrical Safety Foundation International encourages states to adopt NEC 2023, which includes new Article 726 covering Class 4 Fault-Managed Power Systems. ESFI cites that jurisdictions with outdated codes face higher workplace deaths and fire risks.

## Why it matters

Article 726's inclusion in NEC 2023 establishes the first formal code framework for fault-managed power systems, directly affecting AHJ approval processes for electrical contractors and integrators deploying FMP solutions. Jurisdictions still on older NEC editions lack this article, creating code-compliance friction and delaying installations until local adoption occurs.

**Evidence from source:**

- Article 726 — Class 4 Fault-Managed Power Systems is a new Article in NFPA 70, National Electrical Code (NEC), 2023 edition
- ESFI states that jurisdictions with older code versions 'endanger their communities and increase the likelihood of workplace deaths and home fires'
