## What it is

AI data center developers are adopting solid-state transformers to enable direct medium-voltage to 800V DC conversion as rack densities scale from 10 kW to 1 MW per rack. Current AC distribution infrastructure cannot sustain next-generation GPU loads; Nvidia and hyperscalers are driving full-DC architectures that eliminate multiple conversion stages.

## Why it matters

Current data center electrical infrastructure physically cannot sustain the jump from ~100 kW to 1 MW per rack on the same footprint. Facilities managers and electrical designers must evaluate medium-voltage DC distribution and solid-state transformers to replace multi-stage AC conversion paths that bottleneck at high density. This drives fundamental changes in service entrance design, distribution topology, and coordination with utility MV feeds.

**Evidence from source:**

- Current AI data centers: ~10 kW/rack; next generation: ~100 kW/rack; target generation: 1 MW/rack on same footprint.
- Nvidia and hyperscalers pushing 800V DC direct to rack; current AC infrastructure 'physically cannot sustain that.'
- SolarEdge SST targets medium-voltage to DC conversion at 99% efficiency, eliminating multi-stage AC conversion.
