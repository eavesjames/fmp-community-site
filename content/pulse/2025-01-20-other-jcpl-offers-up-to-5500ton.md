## What it is

JCP&L's Building Decarbonization Program offers commercial and industrial customers up to $5,500/ton for qualifying heat pump installations and up to $6,500 for necessary wiring upgrades. The program supports replacement of fossil-fueled equipment with electric systems including air source, VRF, water/ground source heat pumps, electric water heating, and associated electrical infrastructure.

## Why it matters

Wiring upgrade incentives directly address electrical service and panel capacity constraints that often block commercial electrification retrofits. Facilities managers evaluating heat pump conversions can now factor substantial rebates for both equipment and infrastructure into project economics, potentially unlocking projects stalled by panel upgrade costs or service entrance limitations.

**Evidence from source:**

- Up to $6,500 for necessary wiring upgrades explicitly listed alongside equipment incentives
- Program targets commercial and industrial customers replacing fossil-fueled equipment
- Covers multiple heat pump types (air source, VRF, water/ground source) plus electric water heating
