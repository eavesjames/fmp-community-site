## What it is

EnergySage provides 2025 heat pump installation cost benchmarks from real marketplace quotes: national average $15,393, ranging from $8,000 (New Mexico) to $33,000 (New York). Ducted systems average $14,529, ductless multi-zone $25,957, and hybrid heat-pump-plus-furnace $14,353 before incentives. Federal 30% tax credit expired after 2025, shifting focus to state/local programs.

## Why it matters

For facilities managers and contractors scoping building electrification retrofits, the wide cost variance ($8K–$33K) and the ductless premium (multi-zone systems cost more with no economies of scale per added head) directly affect service panel upgrade sizing, electrical capacity planning, and project budgeting. The expiration of the federal tax credit and shift to fragmented state incentives adds estimating complexity and regional variability to electrical load and distribution planning.

**Evidence from source:**

- National average heat pump cost $15,393 before incentives, ranging from $8,000 (New Mexico) to $33,000 (New York) based on EnergySage Marketplace real quotes.
- Ductless mini-splits average $25,957 vs. ducted $14,529; multi-zone ductless offers no significant per-zone discount, driving higher labor and equipment costs.
- Federal 30% tax credit expired for installations after 2025; state and local incentives now primary cost reduction mechanism.
