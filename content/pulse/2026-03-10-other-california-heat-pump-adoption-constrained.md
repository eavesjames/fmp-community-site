## What it is

Article examines California's goal to deploy millions of heat pumps but identifies high electricity bills as a significant adoption barrier. The Building Decarbonization Coalition is mentioned as a stakeholder. Related coverage indicates a 6M unit by 2030 target is stalling on electricity rate vs. gas cost tradeoffs.

## Why it matters

Owner-operators evaluating heat pump retrofits must account for operating cost barriers alongside upfront capital. Policy and rate structure uncertainty affects deployment economics and project approval decisions, particularly when electrical service upgrades may also be required.

**Evidence from source:**

- California 6M heat pump by 2030 target identified
- Electricity rate vs. gas cost tradeoffs creating policy-equipment economics misalignment
- Building Decarbonization Coalition mentioned as stakeholder
