## What it is

Panduit blog interview with Bob Voss explaining UL 1400-1, the first standard developed specifically for Fault Managed Power Systems. Panduit became the first company to earn UL 1400-1 certification, collaborating directly with UL Solutions to define testing procedures, fault detection requirements, and safety thresholds for Class 4 power.

## Why it matters

UL-listed FMPS simplifies AHJ approvals and reduces project timeline risk. Electrical contractors and facility managers gain faster permit approvals and fewer inspection delays when equipment carries UL 1400-1 certification. The standard validates system-level safety including fault detection, power limitation, and thermal management—reducing fire and electrical hazards during installation and operation.

**Evidence from source:**

- UL 1400-1 is the first standard developed specifically for Fault Managed Power Systems, focusing on system-level safety like fault detection, power limitation, and thermal management.
- Panduit became the world's first company to earn UL 1400-1 certification for FMPS, working directly with UL Solutions to shape testing procedures and safety thresholds.
- UL-listed FMP systems simplify permitting and inspections—AHJs are more likely to approve quickly, meaning fewer delays, faster approvals, and less risk for contractors.
