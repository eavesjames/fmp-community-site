## What it is

Zero Homes secured $16.8M Series A to scale software that eliminates in-person Manual J heat pump sizing visits. The platform uses customer-submitted photos/videos to scope systems and generate quotes with rebates, then schedules vetted contractors for installation, cutting project costs 20% average.

## Why it matters

Traditional Manual J assessments require costly in-person visits before homeowners commit, inflating soft costs that contractors must absorb or pass through. Electrical contractors benefit from streamlined scoping that reduces pre-sale expense and accelerates quote-to-install timelines, especially relevant as rising utility costs pressure homeowners to act on electrification projects.

**Evidence from source:**

- Platform cuts heat pump installation cost by 20% on average, with greater savings in rural areas
- Manual J assessment is gold standard for sizing; wrong-size systems cause comfort issues, higher bills, shorter lifespan, mold risk
- In-person visits add time and expense before customers commit, unnecessarily inflating heat pump costs
