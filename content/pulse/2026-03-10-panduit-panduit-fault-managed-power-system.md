## What it is

Panduit's Fault Managed Power System continuously monitors each power circuit and automatically disconnects faulty circuits in under 4 milliseconds while maintaining power to other equipment. The system provides remote monitoring, reduces arc flash hazards compared to traditional breaker panels, and eliminates the need for expensive electrical upgrades in telecommunications rooms and distributed IT infrastructure.

## Why it matters

Traditional power distribution takes down entire branches when a fault occurs, affecting operational equipment. Facilities managers and data center operators gain granular fault management with sub-4ms response times, reducing downtime and arc flash risk while avoiding costly panel upgrades. This directly impacts design decisions around power distribution architecture and installation complexity in telecom rooms and multi-site deployments.
