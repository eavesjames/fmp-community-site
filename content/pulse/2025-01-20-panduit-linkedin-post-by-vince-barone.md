## What it is

Panduit's Vince Barone announces the company delivered the first UL 1400-1 Class 4-certified Fault Managed Power System after collaborating with UL Solutions to develop the certification process itself. The post emphasizes Panduit's role in defining standards rather than just meeting them, with team members celebrating the milestone.

## Why it matters

UL 1400-1 Class 4 certification creates a defined approval pathway for Fault Managed Power deployments, directly addressing AHJ acceptance barriers that have slowed adoption. Electrical contractors and facilities teams can now reference a recognized UL standard during plan review and inspection, reducing project risk and schedule uncertainty. The certification process itself—developed collaboratively—sets precedent for how emerging power distribution technologies gain code compliance.
