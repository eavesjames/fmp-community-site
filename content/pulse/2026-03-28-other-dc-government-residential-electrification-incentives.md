## What it is

DC government page outlines residential electrification incentives (up to $5,000 for heat pumps) and legislative mandates including 100% clean grid by 2032, all-electric net-zero building code adoption in 2026, and no-cost electrification retrofits for 30,000 low-income households by 2040. Programs include free home energy coaching and full electrification for income-qualifying households via DCSEU.

## Why it matters

The 2026 all-electric building code and 30,000-unit retrofit mandate will force service panel upgrades, electrical capacity increases, and workforce scaling in DC's existing housing stock. Electrical contractors and MEP designers must prepare for code transitions affecting new construction and substantial renovations. The no-cost retrofit program for low-income households creates a pipeline of publicly-funded electrification work requiring quality monitoring and contractor certification.

**Evidence from source:**

- DC must adopt an all-electric, net-zero energy code in 2026 (Clean Energy DC Building Code Amendment Act of 2022)
- DC must provide residential electrification retrofits at no cost to 30,000 low-income residents by 2040
- DCSEU offers rebates of up to $5,000 to make the switch from gas to electric, available regardless of income level
