## What it is

Technical comparison of two 2000VA online double-conversion rack UPS systems for network infrastructure and edge computing. Key differentiators include unity power factor output (2000W usable), programmable battery charger up to 12A vs ~1.5-2A typical, scalability to 8 external battery packs, and TAA compliance for government projects.

## Why it matters

IT and facilities teams specifying rack UPS for edge sites face tradeoffs between recharge time (<3 hours with high charge current), runtime scalability (up to 8 external packs), and usable real power (unity PF delivers full 2kW). Faster battery recovery after outages reduces vulnerability windows, while TAA compliance affects procurement for government and critical infrastructure projects.

**Evidence from source:**

- P91g-2000 provides programmable battery charger up to 12A vs ~1.5-2A typical for APC unit, enabling faster recharge with extended batteries
- Unity power factor delivers full 2000W usable output vs 1980W at 2200VA, affecting load density in rack environments
- Scalable to 8 external battery packs with <3 hour recharge time typical, supporting extended runtime requirements
